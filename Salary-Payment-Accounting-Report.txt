Salary Payment - Accounting Report
==================================

The payroll system follows this workflow:

1. Generate salary
   Salary is calculated and saved for the month. No accounting journal entry
   is posted at this stage.

2. Approve salary
   Approval records the salary expense and the amount payable to the employee.

   Journal entry:
     Debit  Salaries & Wages (Account 5020)
       Net salary + salary advance installment
     Credit Advance to Employees (Account 1080)
       Salary advance installment deducted
     Credit Salary Payable (Account 2030)
       Net salary owed to the employee

3. Pay salary
   From the Salary Sheet, select Pay for one salary or Mark Paid for selected
   salaries. Enter the payment date and the cash/bank account. A payment may be
   made in full or in installments.

   Journal entry for each payment:
     Debit  Salary Payable (Account 2030)
       Amount paid
     Credit Selected Cash/Bank Account
       Amount paid

Payment status
--------------

- A partial payment leaves the remaining amount due and the salary status is
  Partially Paid.
- Once the full net salary has been paid, the salary status becomes Paid.
- Payment history records the date, payment account, amount, remarks, and user
  who recorded the payment.
- If an approved salary has no accrual entry yet, the system posts the accrual
  when its first payment is recorded.

Current limitation
-----------------

Advance salary installments are posted to Advance to Employees (1080).
Separate liability accounts and journal entries are not currently described
for tax, provident fund, or other deductions. If those deductions need separate
accounting treatment, define the accounts and add the corresponding posting
logic.
